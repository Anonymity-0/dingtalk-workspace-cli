- **OA approval attachment upload** — `dws oa approval attachment init-upload` initializes an approval attachment upload, returning the OSS upload credential (MCP `oa/init_attachment_upload_info`).
- **OA approval attachment commit** — `dws oa approval attachment commit-upload` commits an uploaded approval attachment to finish the upload flow (MCP `oa/commit_attachment_upload_info`).
